Correction: Quiz 1

Review your answers: Here are the correct answers and explanations based on the phishing lesson.

  • Question 1: Is it possible to imitate perfectly a known email of a big organisation like the "Assurance maladie"?
    Correct answer: Yes it is.
    Explanation: Scammers impersonate organizations by using official logos and creating fake mirror sites to deceive you.

  • Question 2: What is the main goal of a phishing attack?
    Correct answer: To steal personal information.
    Explanation: The hacker's objective is to retrieve your bank details, passwords, or your address for future thefts.

  • Question 3: What is a common tactic used in phishing emails?
    Correct answer: Impersonating a trusted sender with an urgent tone.
    Explanation: A stranger will often pose as a relative in distress or use an alarmist tone to pressure you into clicking a link quickly.

  • Question 4: What is the primary purpose of a phishing email?
    Correct answer: To trick the recipient into using sensitive information and steal it.
    Explanation: Similar to stealing personal information, the scammers want you to actively enter your bank details or passwords on their fake site.

  • Question 5: Are attached files always safe to open?
    Correct answer: No, they can be malicious.
    Explanation: You must absolutely not open attached invoices if you are not expecting them; they often hide spyware programs.

Be careful: Never click on a link in an email that asks for your bank details or passwords!
