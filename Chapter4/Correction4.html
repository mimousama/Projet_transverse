Correction: Quiz 4

Review your answers: Here are the correct answers and explanations based on the AI Voice Cloning lesson.

  • Question 1: How much audio does a scammer currently need to clone your voice using AI?
    Correct answer: Around three seconds of audio.
    Explanation: Three seconds is currently the industry standard for advanced AI models to analyze your unique vocal patterns and generate a realistic synthetic version of your speech.

  • Question 2: Where do scammers typically get the voice samples to clone your voice?
    Correct answer: From public social media videos or by calling you just to get you to speak.
    Explanation: Scammers extract audio from public profiles (like YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram), voicemail greetings, or by calling you from an unknown number just to get you to say "hello".

  • Question 3: What is a common scenario used in an AI voice cloning scam?
    Correct answer: Playing the cloned voice of a loved one in a fake emergency to demand urgent money.
    Explanation: Fraudsters often target parents and grandparents by simulating a crisis (like an accident or arrest) to pressure you into sending money immediately via untraceable methods.

  • Question 4: What is a recommended way to protect yourself from these scams?
    Correct answer: Let calls from unknown numbers go to voicemail and use call screening tools.
    Explanation: Letting calls go to voicemail gives you time to evaluate the situation logically, while call screening tools can act as a questionnaire, asking the caller to state their name and purpose before you answer.

  • Question 5: What should you do if someone claiming to be a relative calls from an unknown number in distress?
    Correct answer: Ask for a pre-established family safe word or hang up and call their known number.
    Explanation: Because the AI scammer does not know your private family safe word, this stops the scam immediately. Alternatively, hanging up and calling the known, trusted number directly bypasses fake Caller IDs.

Be careful: Never share account numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords, PINs, or other sensitive information over the phone if you feel pressured!
