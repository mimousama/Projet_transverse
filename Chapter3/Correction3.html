Correction: Quiz 3

Review your answers: Here are the correct answers based on the quiz on Identity Theft and Family Impersonation.

  • Question 1: What is identity theft?
    Correct answer: It's when someone steals your personal information to commit fraud or other crimes.
    Explanation: For example, a fraudster might steal your information and pose as a relative in distress to obtain an urgent wire transfer.

  • Question 2: How can you protect yourself from identity theft?
    Correct answer: By monitoring your credit reports and financial statements regularly.
    Explanation: Additionally, you should be careful online: do not share family life details or vacation dates on internet profiles visible to everyone.

  • Question 3: What are some signs that your identity might be stolen?
    Correct answer: Noticing unfamiliar accounts or transactions on your credit report.
    Explanation: Unfamiliar transactions can occur if someone fraudulently obtains a wire transfer by posing as you or a relative.

  • Question 4: What is a common method used by identity thieves?
    Correct answer: Stealing personal information from unsecured websites or public places.
    Explanation: Fraudsters often retrieve first names or locations from your social media to appear credible when they contact your loved ones.

  • Question 5: How can the thief use your stolen identity?
    Correct answer: He can't use your voice but can use email, phone number and social security number.
    Explanation: Because they cannot easily mimic a voice, scammers often use a new phone number, claiming the old one was lost or stolen (or that their phone is broken) to justify why you don't recognize the contact.

Be careful: a similar email or one using the name of a relative may be used.
