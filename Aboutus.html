About Elderisk

Helping seniors navigate the digital world safely and confidently.

Technology shouldn't be stressful. Elderisk is a learning platform made specifically for seniors. We skip the complicated technical terms and focus on what really matters: giving you clear, practical advice to protect yourself online.

Our Mission

The internet is a necessary tool for staying connected, managing daily tasks, and learning new things. However, it can also be a place filled with hidden risks.

Elderisk was created with a single goal: to protect our parents and grandparents from online fraud, phishing emails, and malicious text messages by providing accessible, easy-to-understand cybersecurity education.
